01 · The problem

The problem with classical HFT

A classical high-frequency stack is a chain of hand-written stages. Tap a stage to see where the human sits.

Hand-built featuresOrder-flow imbalance, microprice, realized volatility, pair spreads. Each one is a formula a person wrote down. The model can only see the market through them.
Every feature is a human prior.
Every prior is a ceiling
02 · The solution

Orderbook data in. Orders out

No features. The raw exchange feed streams into one model, packet by packet. The model keeps a KV cache of the book's history and emits an order when it reads a critical event. Click a feed type to inject a packet.

      03 · The training stack

      LLM recipe applied to HFT

      The same two-phase recipe that produced today's language models: pretrain on what experts already do, then let the model practice against a simulator until it finds strategies no human wrote down.

      Training pipelineSelf-improvement

      Phase 01 · Imitation learning. Order-book, volatility, pair and sequence alphas that people already trade are the pretraining corpus. The base policy learns to mimic expert trading behavior directly from the raw feed.
