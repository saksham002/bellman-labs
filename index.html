market-native intelligence

A foundation model that trades.

Language models became general by pretraining on raw text. Markets produce a larger, faster, harder stream, and it has no foundation model of its own. We are building it.

SIM · L2 ORDER FLOW · ILLUSTRATIVE

Trained on the market itself.

Today's quantitative pipelines compress the market before a model ever sees it: raw order flow is distilled into hand-engineered signals, and the model's job is reduced to weighing them. Every feature is a human prior, and every prior is a ceiling.

We train on the raw stream — quotes, trades, order flow at tick resolution — and the model outputs trading decisions.

Reinforcement learning closes the loop: the objective is risk-adjusted performance, learned end-to-end against realistic market simulation. The shift that carried language models past feature-engineered NLP applies equally to markets. Executing it demands frontier-scale ML and microstructure fluency in one team.

Three commitments.

Market-native pretraining

A sequence model pretrained on raw, tick-level market data: order books, trades, flow. The representation is learned from the stream itself.

Decision-native output

The model emits actions — quotes, orders, positions — trained with RL against profit and risk. Alpha, sizing, and execution live in one policy.

Deep, not wide

One market, one asset class, mastered first. Depth compounds. Expansion follows once the first market is won.

From thesis to backtested model.

Before this system touches capital, it must earn its way through years of history. The pre-seed round funds that proof.

DATA
Licensed historical market data at tick-level depth — the pretraining corpus. Years of microstructure across the target asset class, the market-native equivalent of a web-scale text crawl.
COMPUTE
Training and simulation infrastructure — pretraining the foundation model, then RL fine-tuning inside a high-fidelity market simulator built from that same historical stream.
VALIDATION
Adversarial backtesting — out-of-sample, regime-split, cost-aware. We hold ourselves to prop-desk standards of evidence.

MILESTONE — a market-native foundation model, specialized to one asset class, with a rigorously backtested trading record. The seed round takes it to market.
