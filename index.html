thesis

Trading models shouldn't read features. They should read the market.

Today's quantitative pipelines compress the market before a model ever sees it: raw order flow is distilled into hand-engineered signals, and the model's job is reduced to weighing them. Every feature is a human prior — and a ceiling.

We train directly on the raw stream — quotes, trades, order flow at the tick level — and the model outputs trading decisions, not forecasts.

Reinforcement learning closes the loop: the objective is not next-token likelihood but risk-adjusted performance, learned end-to-end against realistic market simulation. The same shift that took language models past feature-engineered NLP applies to markets — it simply hasn't been executed yet, because it demands both frontier-scale ML and microstructure fluency in one team.

approach

Three commitments.

Market-native pretraining

A sequence model pretrained on raw, tick-level market data — order books, trades, flow — not on engineered features. The representation is learned, not designed.

Decision-native output

The model emits actions — quotes, orders, positions — trained with RL against profit and risk. No separate alpha, sizing, and execution stack to glue together.

Deep, not wide

One market, one asset class, mastered first. In trading, depth compounds and generality dilutes. Expansion comes after the first market is won.

what pre-seed unlocks

From thesis to backtested model.

We are pre-revenue by design: before this system touches capital, it has to earn its way through history. The pre-seed round buys exactly two things — and a disciplined process around them.

DATA
Licensed historical market data at tick-level depth — the pretraining corpus. Years of microstructure across the target asset class, the market-native equivalent of a web-scale text crawl.
COMPUTE
Training and simulation infrastructure — pretraining the foundation model, then RL fine-tuning inside a high-fidelity market simulator built from that same historical stream.
VALIDATION
Adversarial backtesting — out-of-sample, regime-split, cost-aware. We hold ourselves to prop-desk standards of evidence, not benchmark-paper standards.

MILESTONE — a market-native foundation model, specialized to one asset class, with a rigorously backtested live-trading case. // the seed round takes it to market.
